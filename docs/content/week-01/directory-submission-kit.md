## Positioning variants (do NOT paste the same one everywhere)

**Tagline (<10 words):** Speak Korean before you land in Seoul.

**Short (60 chars):** Scored Korean pronunciation practice for your Seoul trip.

**Long — startup directories (outcome-first, ~90 words):**
HanVoice gets travelers speaking usable Korean before a trip to Korea. Instead
of flashcards, every phrase you say out loud is scored for pronunciation
(per-word accuracy, fluency, completeness), and you rehearse real café, taxi,
restaurant and market conversations with an AI partner that only speaks
Korean. 13 lessons cover the Hangul letters trip phrases need plus the phrases
themselves; 5 conversation scenarios make you use them. Free plan is enough to
start; a one-time $69 Founder Pass unlocks lifetime access.

**Long — AI directories (AI-first, ~80 words):**
HanVoice is an AI speaking coach for Korea-bound travelers. Azure speech
assessment scores every spoken phrase word-by-word; an LLM-driven partner runs
café, taxi, restaurant and market roleplays entirely in Korean, tracks
scenario goals ("ordered a drink", "paid"), and corrects you in context.
Vision models check your handwritten Hangul. It's deliberately narrow: trip
prep, not fluency — the phrases you'll actually say, spoken until an AI can
understand you.

**Long — language-learning niche (~80 words):**
Most Korean apps teach recognition; HanVoice teaches production. You pass a
phrase only by saying it out loud at 60+ pronunciation score. 8 Hangul lessons
cover the 21 letters trip phrases use, 5 Speak lessons drill the phrases, and
5 AI conversation scenarios (café → market) make you use them under mild
pressure. Free daily allowance; $69 lifetime Founder Pass; 14-day refunds.

**Category tags:** language learning, Korean, travel, pronunciation, AI
speaking practice, education, PWA, speech recognition.
